# Subtitle Style Guide

## Audience

The core user is a creator making Xiaohongshu/RedNote content about AI learning, career transition, job hunting, studying abroad, productivity, side projects, or technical storytelling. The subtitles should feel creator-native, not like generic film captions.

## Layout Rules

- Chinese is the primary reading line and should be visibly larger than English.
- English supports bilingual reach but should not compete with the Chinese line.
- Keep both lines in the lower third, but leave enough bottom margin for Xiaohongshu UI overlays.
- Use strong outlines or shadows because mobile feeds compress video heavily.
- Avoid tiny text, thin outlines, low-contrast gray, and long two-line English translations.

## `tech`

Best for:

- AI tutorials
- product demos
- career advice
- technical explainers
- official-looking YouTube videos

Visual intent:

- clean, bright, composed
- white primary text
- restrained cyan/warm-white English support line
- dark outline, minimal shadow
- PingFang-style modern sans font

## `playful`

Best for:

- Xiaohongshu career stories
- study notes
- lifestyle-learning content
- personal transformation videos
- job hunting diaries

Visual intent:

- brighter and more personable
- warm yellow primary text
- white English support line
- thick dark outline for feed readability
- rounded Chinese font if available

## Output Review Checklist

- Can the Chinese line be read on a small phone preview?
- Does the subtitle avoid the creator's mouth and face when possible?
- Is the English line short enough to scan?
- Are punctuation and mixed English terms correct?
- Does the burned MP4 still preserve the original audio?
