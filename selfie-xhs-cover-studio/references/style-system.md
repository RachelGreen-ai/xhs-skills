# Style System

Choose styles by buyer niche and post intent. Do not use all styles at once.

## Pop Tutorial

Use for AI learning notes, tool tutorials, prompt workflows, and beginner guides.

- visual: large portrait cutout, high-saturation background, doodle accents
- colors: yellow, pink, cyan, black, white
- typography: huge outlined Chinese title, short subtitle, badge sticker
- title rhythm: "零基础学AI", "先看这8个工具", "7天搭好路线"

## Notebook Job Hunt

Use for resume, interview, LinkedIn, new-grad, and career-switcher content.

- visual: grid paper, tape, paperclip, checklist labels, creator portrait
- colors: cream, orange, sky blue, navy, white
- typography: bold headline on a paper card, small English helper labels
- title rhythm: "简历别这样写", "投100份没面试？", "面试前先准备这些"

## Creator Skill Launch

Use for Capafy skill sellers, build-in-public posts, product launches, and side-project diaries.

- visual: slanted banner, sticker badges, energetic gradient, large creator cutout
- colors: lime, blue, orange, black, white
- typography: thick outlined title, launch badge, quick outcome subtitle
- title rhythm: "我的第一个AI Skill", "从想法到上架", "这个Skill有人买吗"

## Fresh Study Card

Use for beginner tutorials, tool stacks, saved checklists, and study-note posts.

- visual: green or mint study-card background, grid paper, small labels, friendly portrait
- colors: mint, white, lemon yellow, black, one saturated green
- typography: clean bold title with light outline, checklist subtitle
- title rhythm: "先看这8个工具", "收藏这份路线", "别一开始就学代码"

## AI Side Hustle

Use for build-in-public experiments, tiny products, income tests, and creator workflows.

- visual: bright gradient, handwritten marks, energetic portrait, bold result-focused title
- colors: orange, hot pink, yellow, black, white
- typography: large outlined headline, small proof labels, short subtitle
- title rhythm: "我用AI做了3个小产品", "副业实验第1天", "这个流程跑通了"

## Template Maker

Use for templates, downloadable assets, swipe files, Notion packs, prompt packs, and repeatable workflows.

- visual: cute but organized card layout, grid paper, stickers, productized output preview
- colors: pink, yellow, black, white, one secondary accent
- typography: readable title card, small "template" or "workflow" labels
- title rhythm: "一键生成小红书封面", "这个模板直接套", "自拍+标题+风格包"

## Campus Career

Use for student job search, new-grad tips, LinkedIn makeovers, and interview prep.

- visual: notebook paper, campus-blue palette, tape strip, checklist energy
- colors: sky blue, navy, yellow, white, cream
- typography: bold title, friendly subtitle, practical label
- title rhythm: "LinkedIn这样改", "面试前看这份", "投递前先检查"

## Clean Expert

Use for founders, AI tools, education, and service creators who need a calmer expert look.

- visual: bright portrait, clean studio background, crisp contrast
- colors: white, charcoal, soft red accent, one secondary color
- typography: bold title, small credibility tag, one short subtitle
- title rhythm: "Stop doing X", "3 things I learned", "Before you sign..."

## Soft Lifestyle

Use for personal stories, wellness, career change, parenting, relationships, and creator diaries.

- visual: warm natural light, lifestyle crop, soft background texture
- colors: warm white, rose, muted green, ink black
- typography: softer headline with one light accent
- title rhythm: "I wish I knew this earlier", "Nobody told me...", "My honest experience..."

## Bold Viral

Use for contrarian, mistake, money, business, AI, and checklist posts. Do not promise virality.

- visual: high-contrast portrait cutout or strong background shape
- colors: black, white, red, yellow, electric blue as accents
- typography: short high-impact headline, large numerals, strong hierarchy
- title rhythm: "Do not make this mistake", "Save this before...", "This changed everything"

## Cute Creator

Use for campus life, beauty, outfits, creator lifestyle, dating, and friendly advice.

- visual: bright selfie, sticker-like accents, clean background
- colors: pink, cream, sky blue, lavender, black for text
- typography: rounded bold headline, small badges, light decorative accents
- title rhythm: "My simple routine", "Things I bought again", "Beginner guide"

## Data Proof

Use for SaaS, AI tools, productivity, analytics, investment journaling, and case studies.

- visual: portrait plus charts, app screenshot, dashboard crop, or clean data panel
- colors: white, dark gray, cyan or green accent
- typography: numbered headline, compact labels, table-like microcopy
- title rhythm: "I tested 12 tools", "Results after 30 days", "The exact workflow"

## Selection Rules

- Pick one trust-building style, one emotional style, and one high-click style.
- For AI learning, default to Pop Tutorial, Fresh Study Card, and Bold Viral.
- For job search, default to Notebook Job Hunt, Campus Career, and Bold Viral.
- For Capafy skill creators, default to Creator Skill Launch, AI Side Hustle, and Template Maker.
- For creators, default to Soft Lifestyle, Cute Creator, and Bold Viral.
- For technical or business content, default to Clean Expert, Data Proof, and Bold Viral.

## Cover Composition Rules

- 3:4 canvas, recommended 1242 x 1656.
- Make the face or person visually large; small portraits feel less native on XHS.
- Reserve 25-40 percent of the canvas for title text.
- Use no more than 12 Chinese characters in the main headline when possible.
- Use strong contrast behind text.
- Use outlined titles, badges, tape, sticker shapes, or doodles when the post is educational or career-focused.
- Keep small labels optional; never let them compete with the title.
