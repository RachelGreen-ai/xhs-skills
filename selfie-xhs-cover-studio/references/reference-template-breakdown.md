# Reference Template Breakdown

Use this file when converting creator-provided references into reusable templates.

Important: do not package third-party reference images as final marketplace assets unless the creator confirms usage rights. Do not remove watermarks from references. Extract the craft and rebuild clean original previews.

## Detail Principle

A template feels expensive because of small, repeated decisions:

- title has a distinct shape: slant, overlap, alternating scale, or contour placement
- text has strong readable edges: stroke, shadow, highlight, or glow
- portrait has separation: thick sticker outline, drop shadow, or foreground overlap
- background has texture: grid, photo tiles, blur, paper, dots, diagonal scratches, or sun flare
- small labels feel intentional: short badge, micro English, category tag, date, or hashtag
- decorations follow the composition: arrows, rings, dashed lines, stars, squiggles, not random filler
- safe zones protect the face and the main title from feed cropping

## Reference 1: Collage Sticker Recommendation

Source mood: energetic local recommendation cover with collage tiles, big outlined Chinese title, portrait sticker, and diagonal bottom banner.

Useful details to preserve in the template:

- dark frame makes all inner elements pop
- collage tiles create instant "resource list / recommendation" meaning
- title is split into stacked chunks, tilted slightly, with thick dark outline and bright fill
- portrait overlaps the collage and title area, creating depth
- bottom diagonal banner carries a second hook, not a long sentence
- edge microcopy and repeated small words create density without competing with the main title

Use for:

- AI tool recommendations
- job-search resources
- "save this list" posts
- comparison/roundup content

Template instruction:

Build original abstract/photo-like tiles or use buyer-owned screenshots/photos. Use a thick frame, a sticker portrait, a slanted headline, and one diagonal bottom banner. Add tiny edge microcopy only if it stays secondary.

## Reference 2: Kraft Grid Scrapbook Labels

Source mood: warm scrapbook layout with kraft background, grid paper, cutout subject, tape, badges, and small English strips.

Useful details to preserve:

- kraft tan gives warmth and editorial taste
- white grid-paper center creates a clear writing surface
- portrait is full-body cutout with blue/cream outline
- title sits in a torn-paper or dark banner at the top
- little labels make the design feel handmade: tape strips, clipped notes, repeated small English tags
- color palette stays restrained: kraft, cream, brown, muted blue, sage

Use for:

- route maps
- study plans
- career guides
- travel/campus/lifestyle content

Template instruction:

Use a top title banner, center grid-paper panel, cutout portrait, 2-4 small label stickers, and one optional English strip. Keep the small labels legible but not loud.

## Reference 3: Outdoor Doodle Big Type

Source mood: outdoor green photo with giant seasonal title, playful hand-drawn white doodles, and childlike motion marks.

Useful details to preserve:

- outdoor/photo background feels alive, not like a flat poster
- title is huge and compressed into the top area
- white doodles trace motion and add hand-made charm
- lemon/yellow accents feel optimistic against green
- doodles orbit the subject instead of floating randomly
- small circular text can add polish, but must not become the hook

Use for:

- learning diaries
- monthly recaps
- beginner progress
- motivational posts
- campus/lifestyle stories

Template instruction:

Use a green or outdoor-inspired background, oversized title, 3-6 white doodle marks, and small yellow accents. The title must be readable even if the image is reduced to a feed thumbnail.

## Reference 4: Sunlit Editorial Story

Source mood: sunny personal story cover with elegant large yellow title, natural portrait, soft line accents, and a gentle emotional hook.

Useful details to preserve:

- warm sunlit background gives emotional softness
- large elegant title is placed around the portrait, not isolated in a box
- white arcs/doodles add movement around the person
- small hashtag or English phrase makes it feel social-native
- title color is unified; contrast comes from placement, glow, and background control
- bottom text can exist, but should be short and editorial

Use for:

- personal essays
- transformation stories
- mindset content
- relationship/self-growth posts
- gentle creator diary content

Template instruction:

Use a warm outdoor/photo background, one large portrait, large pale-yellow title chunks around the person, sparse white arcs, and one tiny hashtag. Keep the emotional title short.
