# Buyer Persona

Use this reference before choosing templates, writing prompts, or judging cover quality.

## Primary Buyers

The buyer is usually not a professional designer. They are a creator, student, founder, job seeker, or Capafy skill seller who wants a cover that looks like it belongs on Xiaohongshu without spending an hour in Canva.

Common segments:

- AI learning creators: document tools, prompts, workflows, learning routes, and beginner mistakes
- job-search creators: resume, interview, LinkedIn, career pivot, new-grad, overseas job-search content
- Capafy skill sellers: launch posts, build-in-public notes, "I made this" stories, productized workflow demos
- personal-brand creators: transformation stories, growth notes, study diaries, emotional essays
- recommendation/list creators: tools, city guides, resources, products, checklists

## What They Are Buying

They are not buying "an image." They are buying:

- speed: upload a photo, title, and template choice
- certainty: Chinese title is readable and not hallucinated
- taste: the result does not feel like cheap AI clip art
- native platform fit: it looks plausible in a Xiaohongshu home feed
- repeatability: they can reuse a template for a content series
- identity: their own face makes the post feel personal and credible

## Visual Taste

They tend to prefer:

- large human subject with sticker outline or clear separation from background
- huge title text that survives feed compression
- layered design: background, texture, title, stickers, portrait, doodles
- playful but controlled details: small labels, micro English, tape, dots, arcs, hand-drawn marks
- template styles that feel specific, not generic
- soft premium palettes for career/AI content; louder palettes only for listicles or entertainment

They dislike:

- flat poster layouts with one centered title block
- tiny portraits
- text that sits in boring rows
- obvious AI hallucinated text
- default red/yellow sales-poster palettes unless the topic is intentionally loud
- too much explanation on the cover
- generic gradient backgrounds

## Product Promise

The default workflow should feel like:

1. Upload a photo.
2. Type the exact title.
3. Pick a template style.
4. Get a polished cover direction with title placement, caption hooks, and reusable rules.

Do not make the buyer invent the style from scratch. Show template choices first, then let them customize.
